# UICraft

## Design Context

### Users

Developers migrating valuable TRAE conversation history into OpenCode. They need to understand the workflow quickly, trust that migration is loss-aware, and reach the one-command setup without hunting through documentation.

### Brand Personality

Technical, disruptive, exact. The product should feel like a controlled recovery system rather than a generic AI product or a polished corporate SaaS template.

### Aesthetic Direction

Fracture Protocol: high-contrast editorial composition built from the favicon's own semantics. Emerald represents TRAE, black represents OpenCode, and the central "2" is the fault line where both systems break into and reconstruct one another. Avoid the old TRAE promotional look, generic card grids, gradients, glass effects, neon sci-fi styling, and decorative blobs.

### Design Principles

- Make the break-and-rebuild transition the single memorable visual idea.
- Use real product artifacts: the T2O icon, migration command, terminal output, and verified timeline.
- Keep copy readable and actions obvious even when decorative layers break the grid.
- Reserve emerald for source, transfer, and verified states; use black for the OpenCode target and structural weight.
- Motion should show transfer, scanning, and reassembly with controlled easing; disable spatial motion for reduced-motion users.
